## 事件一：美國聯準會升息一碼

### 當代事實與來源

美國聯邦公開市場委員會於 2026 年 9 月 16 日以 12 比 0 通過升息 25 個基點，把聯邦基金利率目標區間提高至 3.75%–4.00%。官方聲明指出經濟活動穩健擴張，國內支出具韌性，生產力與資本投資強勁，失業率變化不大，但通膨仍偏高。本次措施旨在讓通膨更及時回到 2% 目標。[5]

聯準會的執行說明另列明，準備金利率提高至 3.90%，常備隔夜回購利率為 4.0%，常備隔夜逆回購利率為 3.75%，主要信貸利率提高至 4.0%；相關調整於 2026 年 9 月 17 日生效。[6]

**一手來源一：聯準會 FOMC 聲明。**發布時間為 2026-09-16T14:00:00-04:00，換算後落在本輪窗口；司法管轄區為美國聯邦。[5]

> The Federal Open Market Committee approved the following statement for release by a 12–0 vote. The Committee decided to raise the target range for the federal funds rate by 1/4 percentage point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent. Economic activity is expanding at a solid pace. Domestic spending has been resilient, productivity growth is strong, capital investment is robust, and inflation remains elevated.

**一手來源二：聯準會執行說明。**發布時間同為 2026-09-16T14:00:00-04:00；文件提供操作利率與生效日期。[6]

> The Board of Governors voted unanimously to raise the interest rate paid on reserve balances to 3.90 percent, effective September 17, 2026. The Federal Open Market Committee directed the Desk to maintain the federal funds rate in a target range of 3-3/4 to 4 percent, conduct standing overnight repurchase agreement operations at 4.0 percent, and conduct standing overnight reverse repurchase agreement operations at 3.75 percent.

**獨立來源：CNBC。**發布時間為 2026-09-16T14:00:00-04:00，報導同樣記載 12 比 0、25 個基點及 3.75%–4.00% 的結果。[7]

> The Federal Reserve approved its first interest rate hike in more than three years. The Federal Open Market Committee voted 12-0 to increase its key interest rate by 25 basis points, bringing the overnight funds rate to a target range of 3.75%-4%. The post-meeting statement said inflation remains elevated and that the action would support a timelier return to the Committee's 2 percent goal.

### 來源分歧與邊界

三個來源對已發生的決策、票數及利率區間一致。官方聲明只概括國內需求、生產力、投資、就業與通膨；CNBC 把油價及其他因素列為報導背景，不能轉寫成官方因果認定。媒體所述「可能再升息」屬前瞻判斷，不是已確定政策。

## 事件二：聯合國伊朗事實調查團公布戰爭罪判斷

### 當代事實與來源

聯合國人權理事會授權的伊朗獨立國際事實調查團於 2026 年 9 月 17 日公布最新結果。調查團表示，有合理理由相信美國應對 2026 年 2 月 28 日針對米納卜一所小學，以及拉默爾德體育中心和居民區的兩次攻擊負責；調查團並認為這些不分皂白攻擊可能構成戰爭罪。[8]

**一手來源：聯合國人權事務高級專員辦事處。**頁面只明示 2026-09-17，沒有可核實的發布時分或時區，因此不猜測精確時間。[8]

> The Independent International Fact-Finding Mission on Iran found reasonable grounds to believe that the United States committed war crimes during a pair of airstrikes which killed at least 178 civilians, including women and children. The attacks included Tomahawk missile strikes on the clearly identifiable Shajareh Tayyebeh Primary School in Minab and another strike over a clearly identifiable sports complex and residential area in Lamerd.

**獨立來源一：Reuters。**發布時間為 2026-09-17T04:04:00-04:00，明確落在本輪窗口。報導說明調查團是調查機構而非司法機關，並記載美國中央司令部否認當日攻擊拉默爾德。[9]

> A UN fact-finding mission said it had reasonable grounds to believe the United States was behind two military strikes on a school and sports facility in Iran in February, and that these constituted war crimes. Reuters noted that fact-finding missions are investigative rather than judicial bodies, and reported that U.S. Central Command had denied striking Lamerd that day.

**獨立來源二：Associated Press。**頁面未顯示可核實的發布時分與時區。AP 亦記載兩次攻擊至少造成 178 名平民死亡，並明示報告不具拘束力及調查受到衝突、網路中斷與政府合作不足限制。[10]

> Human rights experts commissioned by the U.N.'s top human rights body said they found reasonable grounds to believe the United States committed war crimes in two strikes in Iran. AP reported that the findings concern a school in Minab and a sports complex and residential area in Lamerd, with at least 178 civilians killed. The report was described as nonbinding but potentially usable in later justice efforts.

### 來源分歧與邊界

OHCHR 與 AP 稱兩案合計至少 178 名平民死亡。OHCHR 分項稱米納卜逾 150 人、拉默爾德 22 人死亡；Reuters 對拉默爾德寫為 22 名平民「死亡及受傷」。AP 另引述伊朗媒體稱米納卜 168 人死亡，但沒有最終清點。這些口徑不能混為同一數字。

調查團的標準是「有合理理由相信」，不是刑事法院的有罪判決。美國中央司令部否認拉默爾德攻擊，美國政府也未直接承認學校攻擊責任。故事件簿只把聯合國調查結論、來源反駁與證據限制並列，不把責任歸屬寫成終局司法事實。
